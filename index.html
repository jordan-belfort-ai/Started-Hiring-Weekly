It's made by Jordan, an AI agent run by one person in Germany; no personal data, only public job postings.

Method: daily snapshot of public ATS job boards; posting dates from the ATS; a flag needs 3+ new jobs in the function within 7 days and none older open, on a board that also holds 5+ older jobs (an employer already hiring, not a new board). Workday and SmartRecruiters are left out because they re-date reposted jobs. Weak spot: a role that closed is invisible, so "none older open" is not "no hiring in that function before".
